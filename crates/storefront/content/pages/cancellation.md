Some items in our store may be offered to you as a subscription. This cancellation policy explains how you can change or cancel these purchases.

## SUBSCRIPTIONS

When you purchase a subscription you will receive repeat deliveries. These are based on the subscription duration and frequency that you select.

Your payment details will be stored securely and you will be charged for each delivery, unless you choose to pay in advance.

Some subscriptions may auto-renew at the end of their duration. If you do not want to renew a subscription you can cancel it.

If you want to cancel or change your subscription, you can do it at any time. Your order confirmation emails contain links to your order, where you can manage your subscription.

## QUESTIONS

If you have any questions about cancellations, please contact us at:

[support@pineappleskinco.com](mailto:support@pineappleskinco.com)
