## WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to the site owners. Cookies can be "first-party" (set by us) or "third-party" (set by other companies whose services we use).

**Similar technologies** include web beacons, pixels, and local storage, which function similarly to cookies and allow us and third parties to track your interactions with our Services.

## WHY DO WE USE COOKIES?

We use cookies and similar technologies for several reasons:

- **Essential functionality** — to keep you logged in, maintain your shopping cart, and protect against fraud
- **Performance and analytics** — to understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it
- **Advertising and marketing** — to deliver relevant advertisements and measure their effectiveness

## HOW CAN YOU CONTROL COOKIES?

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies (other than strictly necessary cookies).

### Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

- **View cookies** stored on your device
- **Delete** individual or all cookies
- **Block** third-party cookies
- **Block** all cookies from specific sites
- **Block** all cookies entirely

Please note that blocking all cookies will affect your experience on our website — you will not be able to log in, maintain a shopping cart, or complete purchases.

Here are links to cookie management instructions for popular browsers:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-and-website-data-sfri11471/mac)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)

### Opt Out of Specific Analytics and Advertising

You can opt out of specific tracking services directly:

- **Google Analytics**: Install the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)
- **Google Ads**: Manage preferences in [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/)
- **Meta (Facebook)**: Manage preferences in [Facebook Ad Preferences](https://www.facebook.com/adpreferences)
- **General advertising opt-out**: Visit the [Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page](http://optout.networkadvertising.org/) or the [Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page](http://optout.aboutads.info/)

### Global Privacy Control (GPC)

We recognize and respond to the [Global Privacy Control](https://globalprivacycontrol.org/) (GPC) opt-out preference signal. When your browser sends the GPC signal, we automatically suppress third-party analytics and advertising scripts for your browsing session. For more information, visit our [Your Privacy Choices](/privacy/data-sharing-opt-out) page.

### Do Not Track

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. For more information on how we respond to DNT signals, see the "Controls for Do-Not-Track Features" section of our [Privacy Policy](/privacy#9-controls-for-do-not-track-features).

## WHAT ABOUT LOCAL STORAGE?

In addition to cookies, some of our services use browser localStorage to store data on your device. Unlike cookies, localStorage data is not sent to the server with each request.

| Key | Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Mixpanel identifiers | [Mixpanel](https://mixpanel.com/legal/privacy-policy/) | Stores anonymous device identifiers for product analytics. Mixpanel primarily uses localStorage instead of cookies. |

You can clear localStorage through your browser's developer tools or by clearing all site data in your browser settings.
