As described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy), we collect personal information from your interactions with us and our website, including through cookies and similar technologies. We may also share this personal information with third parties, including advertising partners. We do this in order to show you ads on other websites that are more relevant to your interests and for other reasons outlined in our privacy policy.

Sharing of personal information for targeted advertising based on your interaction on different websites may be considered "sales", "sharing", or "targeted advertising" under certain U.S. state privacy laws. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to opt out of these activities.

## How to Opt Out

### Global Privacy Control (GPC)

The simplest way to opt out is to enable [Global Privacy Control](https://globalprivacycontrol.org/) (GPC) in your browser. When we detect the GPC signal, we automatically suppress third-party analytics and advertising scripts for your browsing session.

GPC is supported in browsers and extensions including:

- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/global-privacy-control) (built-in)
- [Brave](https://brave.com/) (built-in)
- [DuckDuckGo](https://duckduckgo.com/app) browser and extension
- [Privacy Badger](https://privacybadger.org/) extension

For a full list of supported browsers and extensions, visit [globalprivacycontrol.org](https://globalprivacycontrol.org/).

### Submit a Data Request

If you prefer not to use GPC, you can submit an opt-out request through our [Data Request](/data-request) page. Select "Opt out of targeted advertising" or "Opt out of data sharing" as the request type, and we will process your request in accordance with applicable laws.

### Manage Cookies Directly

You can also control third-party cookies through your browser settings or by opting out of specific advertising platforms. See our [Cookie Notice](/privacy/cookies#how-can-you-control-cookies) for detailed instructions.
