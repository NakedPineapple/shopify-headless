## How do I know which products are right for my skin type?

Check out our [Starter Kit](/products/starter-kit) for a complete introduction to our core products. Each product page also includes skin type recommendations. Not sure? [Contact us](/contact)—we love helping you find your perfect routine!

## What skin types do your products work for?

Our products are formulated to work across all skin types—oily, dry, combination, and sensitive. Each product page notes which skin types it's best suited for.

## How much product should I use?

A little goes a long way! Most of our products work best with a pea-sized to dime-sized amount. Check the product packaging or product page for specific usage recommendations.

## What order should I apply my products?

For the best results, follow this general order:

1. **Cleanser** — Start with a clean canvas
2. **Toner** — Prep and balance your skin
3. **Serum** — Target specific concerns
4. **Moisturizer** — Lock in hydration
5. **Sunscreen** (AM only) — Protect your glow
