## Are your products cruelty-free?

Yes! We are proudly cruelty-free and never test on animals. We believe in beauty without harm.

## Are your products vegan?

Most of our products are vegan. Check individual product pages for specific ingredient information.

## What makes pineapple so great for skin?

Pineapple is packed with bromelain, a natural enzyme that gently exfoliates and brightens skin. It's also rich in vitamin C and antioxidants that help protect and nourish your skin.

## Are your products safe for sensitive skin?

Our formulas are designed to be gentle yet effective. However, we always recommend doing a patch test before using any new product, especially if you have sensitive skin.

## Where can I find the full ingredient list?

Full ingredient lists are available on each product's page. If you have specific allergy concerns, please contact us and we'll be happy to help.
