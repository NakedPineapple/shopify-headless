## Where do you ship?

We currently ship throughout the United States. We're working on expanding to international destinations—stay tuned!

## How long does shipping take?

- **Standard Shipping:** 5-7 business days
- **Expedited Shipping:** 2-3 business days

Processing time is typically 1-2 business days before your order ships.

## How much does shipping cost?

- **Free shipping** on orders over $50
- **Standard Shipping:** $5.99
- **Expedited Shipping:** $12.99

## How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with tracking information. You can also log into your account to view your order status and tracking details.
