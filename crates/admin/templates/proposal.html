Customer Support

Every Customer Question Gets an Answer

A complete customer support system built into the admin panel — powered by Klaviyo. No more jumping between info@nakedpineapple.co, support@nakedpineapple.co, and whatever other inboxes have accumulated over the years. Every conversation lives in one place, AI handles the noise, and the messages that actually need you rise to the top.

All Your Inboxes, One Screen

Every email account — info@, support@, and any others — feeds into a single unified inbox in the admin panel. AI actively monitors incoming messages and responds to the ones it can handle on its own. What's left are the conversations that genuinely need your attention, surfaced clearly with no noise around them.

Customer Ticketing

Every customer inquiry — whether it comes from email, the website, or any other channel — becomes a ticket you can track, assign, and resolve from the admin panel. Nothing gets lost, and you can see the full history of every customer interaction at a glance.

Live Chat on Your Website

Customers can start a conversation directly from the storefront without sending an email or picking up the phone. Questions about shipping, ingredients, order status — they just ask. You respond from the admin panel, all in one thread.

AI-Powered Instant Answers

Common questions — "Where's my order?", "What's in this product?", "Do you ship to Canada?" — are answered instantly and automatically by AI, using your real product data and store policies. Customers get immediate help, and you only step in when a human touch is needed.

Customer Hub

Your customers get a self-service portal where they can check order status, browse past purchases, manage their subscription, and find answers to common questions — all without contacting you. Fewer inbound messages, happier customers.

Single pane of glass — for support too

This isn't a separate tool you log into. Customer conversations appear right alongside your orders, products, and analytics in the admin panel. When a customer asks about an order, their purchase history and ticket history are on the same screen. No tab-switching, no copy-pasting order numbers between systems.

Analytics & Intelligence

Data-Driven Decisions

Questions like "Am I actually making money?", "What does it cost me to make this product?", and "Which products are most profitable?" shouldn't be hard to answer — but without the right tools, they are. This platform puts all of your financial and performance data in one place so you can see the health of your business at a glance, without a finance degree and without digging through spreadsheets.

Sales Dashboard

Total sales, net sales, orders, units, and average order value — all in plain language, updated daily. See how each sales channel contributes to revenue and spot trends over any date range. No formulas, no exports — just open the dashboard.

Cost of Goods

Every manufacturing batch is tracked — raw materials, labels, cartons, labor — and broken down to a cost per unit. When a product sells, the platform automatically knows what it cost to make. This is the number most small businesses are missing, and it's the difference between thinking you're profitable and knowing it.

Profit Margins

Because the platform tracks both what you sell things for and what they cost to make, it can calculate your actual profit — per product, per order, and across the whole business. You'll always know which products make you the most money and which ones are quietly losing it.

Storefront Tracking

Tracking across 10 ad platforms captures every customer interaction — so you can see exactly which channels drive revenue, show ads to past visitors, and find similar customers. Every dollar you spend on advertising can be traced back to actual sales.

Automation

If It Can Be Automated, It Will Be

The guiding philosophy is simple: you should never have to do something repetitive that a machine can do for you. Every workflow below runs on its own — you only get involved when a decision genuinely requires a human.

Customer Emails

Order confirmations, shipping updates, delivery notifications, review requests, subscription reminders — all sent automatically at exactly the right moment. You never have to remember to follow up with a customer.

Abandoned Cart Recovery

When a customer adds products to their cart but doesn't check out, the system automatically sends a personalized reminder. This recovers revenue that would otherwise be lost — without you lifting a finger.

Customer Support Triage

AI reads every incoming customer message. Questions it can answer — "Where's my order?", "What are the ingredients?" — it handles immediately. Everything else is sorted by urgency so the most important conversations reach you first.

Low Stock Alerts

When inventory for any product drops below a threshold, you're notified automatically. No more manually checking stock levels or discovering you've sold out after a customer tries to order.

Customer Segmentation

Customers are automatically grouped based on their behavior — first-time buyers, repeat purchasers, VIPs, lapsed customers, subscribers. These segments power targeted email campaigns without you having to manually sort through a spreadsheet.

Reporting & Summaries

Daily and weekly business summaries — revenue, orders, top products, marketing performance — are compiled and delivered automatically. You start each day knowing how the business is doing without running a single report.

Cross-Channel Sync

When you update a product price, description, or image in the admin panel, the change is pushed to Shopify and flows out to every connected sales channel automatically. One edit, everywhere at once.

Subscription Lifecycle

Upcoming renewal reminders, payment failure notifications, and win-back emails for canceled subscribers all run automatically. The system handles the entire subscription relationship from signup to renewal to re-engagement.

This list will grow

Every time you find yourself doing the same task twice, that's a candidate for automation. As we work together, I'll keep identifying repetitive work and building it into the platform so you can focus on the parts of the business that actually need your judgment and creativity.

Private Access

Tools & Services

What Powers the Platform

The platform connects to a handful of best-in-class services. Here's what each one does and why it's there.

Core

Shopify

The backbone. Manages your product catalog, processes checkout and payments, tracks inventory, and handles order data. You already use this today.

Fly.io

Hosts both applications (storefront and admin) in the cloud. Provides separate testing and live environments so nothing goes live untested.

Cloudflare

Delivers images from servers closest to each visitor for fast loading. Also protects the site from attacks and provides global caching.

Tailscale

Creates a private network so the admin panel is completely hidden from the public internet. Only authorized team members can access it.

Marketing & Communications

Klaviyo

Email marketing, SMS campaigns, and customer support — all in one platform. Sends newsletters, manages subscriber lists, powers automated sequences, and provides customer ticketing, live chat, AI-powered instant answers, and a self-service customer hub.

Slack

Receives notifications from the platform. When the AI assistant wants to make a change, the approval request appears in Slack for you to review.

Intelligence & Monitoring

Anthropic (Claude AI)

Anthropic's AI powers two things. First, the built-in assistant in your admin panel that can answer questions, generate reports, and prepare changes for your approval. Second, Claude Code Max — an AI development partner that works directly inside the codebase to build features, fix issues, and maintain the platform. Think of it as a senior engineer on call around the clock for a fraction of what that would normally cost.

OpenAI

Powers the product search engine. Converts product names and descriptions into numerical representations so the site can find what customers are looking for, even with imprecise searches.

Sentry

Automatic error monitoring. If something goes wrong on the site, Sentry captures the details immediately so it can be diagnosed and fixed quickly.

Better Stack

Centralized logging for both applications. Every request, error, and system event is recorded and searchable — making it fast to diagnose issues, trace problems back to their source, and understand exactly what happened and when.

Mosyle (Fuse)

Apple device management. Keeps your business Macs, iPhones, and iPads secure and configured — automatic updates, security policies, and identity management from one dashboard. This is required in order to ensure devices accessing the admin panel are secure.

Analytics & Behavior

Mixpanel

Product analytics that tracks how customers actually use the storefront — which pages they visit, where they drop off, how they navigate from browsing to purchase. Goes deeper than Google Analytics by following individual user journeys over time.

Crazy Egg

Visual behavior tracking. Heatmaps show exactly where customers click, how far they scroll, and what they ignore — so you can see what's working on each page and what needs to change. Like watching customers shop without being creepy.

What This Would Cost to Build

This is not a standard website. In industry terms, it's a lightweight OMS (Order Management System — software that tracks orders, inventory, and fulfillment across all your sales channels) combined with an ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning — software that ties together financials, manufacturing costs, and business operations into one system). Platforms like these typically cost tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars. Here's how the market prices this kind of work.

Pro Bono

This project is provided at no cost.

The market rates below are included so you can see the value of what you're receiving. This entire platform — design, engineering, integrations, AI, and ongoing infrastructure — is built pro bono. Your only costs are the third-party services listed in the previous section.

Why This Isn't Priced Like a Website

A typical freelance website — even a nice Shopify store — runs $8,000 to $20,000. That covers a theme, some product pages, and a checkout. What you're looking at here is fundamentally different:

  • A completely custom storefront (not a theme)
  • A full admin panel built from scratch — 20 management areas
  • An AI assistant that understands your business data across 15 areas
  • Warehouse integration, manufacturing cost tracking, and profit margin reporting
  • Ad tracking across 10 platforms, email/SMS marketing, and analytics dashboards
  • Bank-level security with private network access

The cost is driven by the number of business systems it replaces and how deeply they're connected — not by how many pages it has.

Working Together

My Ongoing Role & How We Communicate

What to expect from me, how to reach me, and where the boundaries are.

The single rule that defines what I do and don't do

The goal of this project is one screen for everything. If something can be brought into this admin panel, I will build it, connect it, and maintain it. If something cannot be brought in and has to be managed in its own separate service, that falls to you. The whole point is to eliminate the need to jump between platforms — so my job is to keep expanding what's possible from this single dashboard.

What I Handle

Platform Maintenance

Keeping everything running smoothly — software updates, security patches, and making sure all the connected services stay in sync. If something breaks, I fix it.

New Features & Integrations

If there's something you wish the admin panel could do — a new report, a smoother workflow, a new service to connect — ask. I'll research whether it can be integrated, and if it can, I'll build it.

Monitoring & Reliability

Automated error tracking alerts me when something needs attention. I proactively monitor performance and address issues — often before you even notice them.

Integration Upkeep

Shopify, Amazon, Faire, Klaviyo, and the other connected services occasionally change how they work. When that happens, I update the platform to stay compatible.

How You Interact With Me

Ask the AI Anything

The AI assistant in the admin panel is your first stop for any question about your store — sales numbers, order status, inventory levels, customer trends, marketing performance, anything. Just ask in plain English. I review every conversation, so if the assistant can't answer something today, I'll teach it to answer that question going forward. Over time, it gets smarter about your business specifically.

Request Board

The admin panel includes a built-in request board where you can submit bug reports and feature requests. Each request is tracked from submission through completion, so you always know the status. This is the best way to ask for changes — it keeps everything organized and nothing gets lost.

Slack Notifications

The platform sends automatic notifications to Slack when things need attention — like AI-suggested changes awaiting approval. This keeps us both in the loop without extra effort.

When in Doubt, Just Ask

Not sure if something can be added to the platform? Ask. I may not know off the top of my head either, but I'll research it. Many services offer ways to connect that aren't obvious — and if there's a way to bring it into your dashboard, I'll find it and build it.

What Falls Outside My Scope

The flip side of the single-screen rule: anything that has to live in another platform's own interface is yours to manage there.

  • Running your business — Fulfilling orders, responding to customers, managing inventory counts, running promotions. The platform makes all of this easier, but the day-to-day operations are your domain.
  • Platform-specific management — Logging into Amazon Seller Central, TikTok, Facebook, Faire, or any other service to manage settings, policies, or account details within their own interface. If the action can be done from the admin panel, I own it. If it can only be done in their interface, that's on you.
  • Content and ads — Writing product descriptions, photography, creating ad campaigns, and similar creative work — unless that work is directly integrated into this platform (for example, email campaigns managed through the Klaviyo integration are fair game).
  • Business strategy — Pricing decisions, which products to stock, when to run sales, which channels to prioritize. I can build the reports and dashboards that help you decide, but the decisions are yours.
  • Data corrections — Syncing between services is best-effort. When mismatches happen (an inventory count is off, an order didn't sync), you handle the manual correction through the admin panel.

The short version

If it's in the admin panel, it's my responsibility. If it's not in the admin panel but could be, ask me and I'll look into it. If it can only be done inside another service's own website, that's yours. The platform gets more capable over time — and so does the AI assistant — because I'm always watching for gaps to fill.
