Monthly Cost

What It Costs to Run

These are the third-party service costs to keep the platform running. Prices are approximate and based on current published rates — most scale with usage.

Service What You Pay Notes
Shopify $39/mo Basic plan (or $29/mo if paid annually). You already pay this.
Fly.io $50/mo Hosts both apps + databases across staging and production. Includes compute, storage, and bandwidth.
Cloudflare $20/mo Pro plan. Firewall with managed attack rulesets, bot blocking, image optimization, caching, and content delivery.
Cloudflare ACM $10/mo Advanced Certificate Manager. Shorter-lived security certificates, custom certificate authority, and deeper subdomain coverage.
Cloudflare R2 $5/mo Image storage at $0.015/GB/month. No fees for image delivery (zero egress charges). First 10 GB free.
Tailscale $6/user/mo Starter plan. Private network for admin access. First 3 users included, then $6 per additional user.
Klaviyo $124/mo Email ($45), SMS ($15), reviews ($25), customer hub ($20), helpdesk ($10), plus tax. Scales with subscriber count.
Slack Free Free plan is sufficient for approval notifications.
Anthropic — Admin AI Assistant $20/mo Pay per conversation. Powers the built-in assistant in your admin panel. Light usage is very affordable; scales with how much you use it.
Anthropic — Claude Code Max $200/mo AI-powered development tool used to build, maintain, and improve your platform. Replaces what would otherwise be billable engineering hours — a senior developer typically costs $100–150+/hour, so this pays for itself many times over every month.
OpenAI $1/mo Search embeddings. Extremely low cost — fractions of a penny per product indexed.
Sentry $29/mo Team plan. Automatic error monitoring, alerts, and performance tracking.
Better Stack $24/mo Centralized logging for both applications. Searchable request and error logs for fast diagnosis.
Mixpanel Free Free plan includes up to 20M events/month. Tracks user journeys, funnels, and retention across the storefront.
Crazy Egg $29/mo Standard plan. Heatmaps, scroll maps, and click tracking to see how customers interact with every page.
Mosyle Fuse $45/mo Apple device management at $1.50/device/month for up to 30 devices. Covers security policies, updates, and identity. This is required to ensure devices accessing the admin panel are secure.
GitHub Free Hosts the code and runs automated deployments.
