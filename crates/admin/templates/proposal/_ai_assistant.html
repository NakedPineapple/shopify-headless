Safety: Nothing Changes Without Your OK

The AI assistant can do more than answer questions — it can take action on your behalf. Refund a customer, update a product price, cancel an order, adjust inventory, apply a discount — anything you can do in the admin panel, you can ask the AI to do instead. It will never make a change without confirming with you first.

Example: You say "Refund order #1042 for the damaged moisturizer." The AI looks up the order, calculates the refund amount, and sends you a summary in Slack: "Refund $34.99 to Jane Smith for order #1042?" — with Approve and Deny buttons. Nothing happens until you tap Approve.
